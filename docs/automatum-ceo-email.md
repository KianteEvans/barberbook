Ahead of our next session, here is a summary of the four solutions we've packaged under the BOX program, and the short list of decisions we need from Automatum to start sequencing submissions.

**How the portfolio is built.** There are four jobs an ISV's AWS business needs staffed — getting the product listed, running the AWS partnership, operating the Marketplace, and building the reseller channel — and the portfolio covers them with one fixed-fee project and three twelve-month retainers. Automatum leads every solution, owns the listed software, and is seller of record; OBP delivers the engagement. Each customer signs one agreement and transacts through AWS Marketplace on Automatum's listing — software on one line, professional services on the other — and each solution carries its own Solution ID and milestone submission.

**The four solutions:**

1. **Marketplace Compliance Accelerator** (fixed-fee project) — takes a product from unassessed to a published, transacting AWS Marketplace listing in 14–18 business days of delivery. $10,000 fixed: 50% on signature, 50% on completion, with 20 hours of remediation included and further 10-hour blocks at $1,000.

2. **Managed AWS Alliances** (retainer) — puts a named owner on the AWS partnership: OBP serves as the ISV's Director of Alliances for twelve months, across three strictly additive tiers (Essentials, Growth, Professional).

3. **Managed Marketplace Operations** (retainer) — operates a live listing at volume: nine services in three groups at every tier, with depth the only axis between rungs (Launch, Operate, Scale).

4. **Managed Partner Development** (retainer) — builds and runs a reseller channel: partners recruited, enabled, and operated on an unlimited roster, with partner types setting the tiers (Establish, Expand, Accelerate).

All three retainers price on the same ladder: $2,500 / $5,000 / $7,500 per month. Each solution stands alone in front of a customer, with its own overview, deck, Solution ID, and milestone submission — the ladder is the one thing they share.

**What the program funds, and what AWS gets back.** Milestone 1 releases $10,000 cash plus $10,000 in credits, Milestone 2 $25,000 plus $25,000, and Milestone 3 is AWS-funded on 55 confirmed project leads. In return, AWS gets four solutions listed and transacting on Automatum's listing, twenty ACE opportunity submissions (five per solution, with each opportunity belonging to exactly one solution), and a named alliance owner on every engagement.
