track the included offer band rather than the seniority of the person on the account. At $18,000 over
the term against $10,000, the retainer still isn't the cheap way in, which is what makes the pair
coherent. The Accelerator page now says why it exists alongside.

retire; it doesn't. Recruiting and managing a roster is our own act, so 6 / 12 / uncapped is
committed. Partners issuing offers remains the headline we report, as you asked. And the 8-month ramp
from first outreach to first partner transaction is now on the slide as the reason the term is twelve
months — the first stated reason in the portfolio for a customer to sign for a year.

participant. Both slides now say which one they describe, so nobody reads it as double-counting.

The sequence is two waves — Accelerator plus Alliances first, then Operations plus Partner
Development, ten ACE submissions per wave, wave two opening on wave one's Milestone 1 approval. It
follows your value chain down the middle.
