# BOX Solutions deck — revision spec

**Source deck:** `Automatum_x_OBP__BOX_Solutions_Internal.pdf` — 14 slides, internal, dated 2026-08-30.
**Trigger:** Aein's (Automatum CEO) reply to our solutions email.
**Status:** spec only. Whoever holds the `.pptx` executes it.

This document is written to be executed slide by slide. Every edit to an existing slide quotes the
current wording. Every new slide is drafted in full. Every unset number is a bracketed placeholder
with an owner in the register at the end — **nothing in this spec should reach a customer artifact
while it still contains a bracket.**

## 1. What Aein actually objected to

> "The Accelerator has a clear one — a published, transacting listing in 14 to 18 business days. For
> Alliances, Operations and Partner Development, what is the customer actually being promised at
> twelve months? The tier names use outcome language — build pipeline, close deals, run a producing
> channel — but I can't see the numbers sitting behind them."

**This is a layout problem, not a wording problem.** The deck contains exactly one contract-shaped
object: p7's Term / What-it-covers table (fee, payment, remediation, delivery). It belongs to the
Accelerator — a $10,000 one-off. The three retainers, which carry all the recurring revenue and all
the twelve-month risk, share a single slide where each rung is three words in a pricing grid.

| | Slides | Terms table | Per-rung detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketplace Compliance Accelerator | 1 (p7) to itself | Yes | n/a — fixed-fee project |
| Managed AWS Alliances | ⅓ of p8 | No | 3-word label |
| Managed Marketplace Operations | ⅓ of p8 | No | 3-word label |
| Managed Partner Development | ⅓ of p8 | No | 3-word label |

Alliances at its middle rung is $60,000 over twelve months — six times the Accelerator's fee.
The deck's information architecture tells the reader the project is a product and the retainers are
a price list.

Two aggravators sit underneath it:

1. **The deck's spine is price, not work.** p1 is titled *"Four services, one price ladder"*; p4's
   footer, p5, p8's grid, p10 and p12 all argue sameness. Only p7 argues content. A deck whose
   organising claim is "these four are the same underneath", which then supplies substance for one
   of them, hands the reader Aein's *"are these genuinely four solutions?"* question for free.
2. **The p7 table encodes bounded scope at a price** — 20 hours included, further blocks of 10 at
   $1,000. The retainers have no included-volume concept anywhere, so any commit column written for
   them is an uncapped promise at a fixed fee.

**The fix is parity of artifact.** Every solution gets the same five slots — commit, target, band,
customer inputs and clock start, evidence — with the retainers' slots filled per rung.

### Two BOX participations, one AWS schedule — settled

The Alliances outcomes carry 35k in AWS credits + 35k in cash and 55 leads. Those are *exactly*
Milestone 1 ($10k+$10k) plus Milestone 2 ($25k+$25k) and the 55 leads on the exchange slide. **They
are not the same money.** There are two separate participations running the same AWS programme
schedule:

- **Ours** — the four Automatum × OBP solutions, their Solution IDs, their milestones (slide 19).
- **The ISV customer's** — their own BOX Program listings, which the Alliances retainer drives them
  toward (slide 11).

Confirmed by OBP. Both slides must name which participation they describe; identical figures with no
explanation reads as double-counting, and would be picked apart on the call.

### Every ask, and where it is answered

Use this as the completion checklist — nothing may be left unmapped.

## 5. The strongest objection, and where it gets answered

**Getting listed appears in three of the four solutions** — the Accelerator ($10,000 fixed,
14–18 business days), Alliances *Essentials*, and Operations *Launch*, which now explicitly includes
WAFR, FTR and publication at $1,500/month. This is the version of *"are these genuinely four

shared-ladder version Aein raised.

**Decided: Operations absorbs the listing work, and the Accelerator stays.** The two are different

| | What it is | Twelve-month cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Accelerator** | The fixed-fee route to a published listing in 14 to 18 business days, nothing ongoing | $10,000 |
| **Operations Launch** | The same listing work, then twelve months operating the listing | $18,000 |

**"Why buy it standalone"** row; slides 5 and 16 carry the boundary; Alliances is scoped to the AWS
partnership programme and the ISV's own BOX Program listings, not Marketplace listing work.
