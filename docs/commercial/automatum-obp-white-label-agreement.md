# White Label Delivery Agreement

**Managed Partner Development**

This White Label Delivery Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of
[to supply: Effective Date] (the "Effective Date") by and between:

**[to supply: Automatum legal entity name]**, a [to supply: entity type] organized under the
laws of [to supply: state of organization], with company number [to supply: registration
number] and principal place of business at [to supply: Automatum principal address]
("**Automatum**"); and

**[to supply: Only Best Practices legal entity name]**, a [to supply: entity type] organized
under the laws of [to supply: state of organization], with company number [to supply:
registration number] and principal place of business at [to supply: OBP principal address]
("**OBP**").

Automatum and OBP are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

## Recitals

**A.** Automatum publishes software through AWS Marketplace, holds the seller of record
position for its listing, and issues private offers and Selling Authorizations under that
listing.

**B.** OBP operates a managed channel function comprising reseller recruitment, partner
contracting, enablement and training, a Channel Partner Private Offer deal desk, and channel
governance, and licenses and operates a partner system of record supporting partner
onboarding, deal registration and commission tracking.

**C.** The Parties have jointly developed a solution called Managed Partner Development, under
which an independent software vendor with a live AWS Marketplace listing engages a single
provider to build and run its reseller channel on AWS. The solution is the subject of a
feasibility study submitted to the AWS Business Outcomes Xcelerator program.

**D.** The Parties intend that Automatum contracts with, invoices and faces each client, and
that OBP performs the channel function as a subcontractor to Automatum under Automatum's
brand, such that OBP is not presented to the client as a separate provider.

**E.** The Parties intend this arrangement to be non-exclusive, and each Party remains free to
pursue comparable arrangements with third parties, subject to the restrictions set out in
this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows.

## 3. Appointment

**3.1 Appointment.** Automatum appoints OBP, and OBP accepts appointment, as a delivery
subcontractor to perform the Services for Registered Clients under the Automatum Brand, on
the terms of this Agreement.

**3.2 Non-exclusive.** The appointment is non-exclusive in both directions. Nothing in this
Agreement prevents:

  a. Automatum from performing the Services itself, or from appointing any other person to
     perform services that are the same as or similar to the Services, for any client; or
  b. OBP from providing services that are the same as or similar to the Services to any other
     person, including to another AWS Marketplace seller.

  This clause 3.2 is subject to clause 15 (Non-Circumvention and Non-Solicitation), which
  operates only in respect of Registered Clients and Recruited Partners.

**3.3 No volume commitment.** Automatum does not commit to any minimum number of Engagements,
Registered Clients or Client Fees. OBP acknowledges that it has not relied on any forecast,
including any projection in the feasibility study referred to in Recital C, in entering into
this Agreement.

**3.4 No agency.** OBP is an independent contractor. OBP is not Automatum's agent, employee,
partner or joint venturer, and has no authority to enter into any commitment, make any
representation, incur any liability or accept any obligation on Automatum's behalf, except as
Automatum expressly authorizes in writing in respect of a specific matter.

**3.5 Prime responsibility.** As between the Parties and each Registered Client, Automatum is
the prime contractor and is responsible to the Registered Client for the Solution as a whole.
As between the Parties, OBP is responsible for the Services and for the acts and omissions of
its personnel and subcontractors in performing them.

## 4. Client Registry and Engagement Orders

**4.1 Purpose.** Because the appointment is non-exclusive, the Client Registry is the sole
record of which Clients are subject to this Agreement. A Client is within the scope of this
Agreement only once it is entered in the Client Registry.

**4.2 Adding a Client.** A Client is added to the Client Registry when the Parties execute an
Engagement Order for that Client. Automatum maintains the Client Registry and provides OBP
with a current copy on request and in any event with each monthly statement under clause 11.6.

**4.3 Engagement Order contents.** Each Engagement Order states at least: the Client's legal
name; the Tier; the Engagement start date and Engagement Year dates; the Client Fees; the
resulting OBP Service Revenue calculated under clause 11.2; the named personnel required by
clause 9; the PSR seat allocation; and any variation to this Agreement permitted by clause
2.3(a).

**4.4 Prior relationships.** Before an Engagement Order is executed, OBP may notify Automatum
in writing that it has a pre-existing relationship with the proposed Client or with a named
partner of that Client, giving reasonable evidence of that relationship. A relationship so
notified and evidenced is a "Prior Relationship" and is carved out of clause 15.2 in respect
of that Client or partner. A relationship not notified before the Engagement Order is
executed is not a Prior Relationship.

**4.5 Refusal.** OBP may decline a proposed Engagement Order by notice given within five (5)
Business Days of receiving it, acting reasonably, including where OBP lacks capacity to meet
the service levels for the proposed Tier or where the proposed Client competes with an
existing OBP client. Declining does not breach this Agreement. Automatum may then perform the
Services itself or appoint another provider for that Client, and that Client is not a
Registered Client.

**4.6 Removal.** A Client remains in the Client Registry while its Engagement is in effect and
for the tail period stated in clause 15.4, after which it is marked closed. Closure does not
affect obligations that survive under clause 23.6.

## 5. OBP Delivery Obligations

**5.1 The Services.** For each Engagement, OBP will perform the Services described in Schedule
1 for the applicable Tier, in accordance with the service levels in Schedule 4, using suitably
skilled and experienced personnel, with reasonable skill and care.

**5.2 Partner recruitment.** OBP will identify, qualify, recruit and onboard Recruited
Partners for each Registered Client up to the number stated for the applicable Tier in
Schedule 1.

**5.3 Recruitment is a ceiling, not a minimum.** The partner numbers in Schedule 1 are the
maximum number of Recruited Partners OBP is obliged to recruit and onboard at each Tier. OBP
does not warrant, and is not obliged to procure, that any minimum number of partners is
recruited, onboarded, authorized, transacts, or becomes an Active Partner. Automatum will not
represent otherwise to any Client. This clause 5.3 prevails over any contrary reading of
Schedule 1.

**5.4 Partner contracting.** OBP will draft, issue and administer the partner agreement and
margin schedule for each Registered Client's channel. Each partner agreement is executed
between the Registered Client, or Automatum acting as that Client's agent, and the Recruited
Partner. **OBP is not a party to any partner agreement as principal** and will not enter into
any partner agreement in its own name. OBP administers partner agreements as agent, under the
limited authority granted in the relevant Engagement Order and no wider.

**5.5 Enablement and training.** OBP will deliver partner enablement and training sessions at
the cadence stated for the applicable Tier in Schedule 1.

**5.6 Deal desk.** OBP will operate the CPPO deal desk, receiving Deal Desk Requests and
delivering an Issuance-Ready Package to Automatum within the OBP leg of the applicable service
level in Schedule 4. OBP's deal desk obligation ends on delivery of the Issuance-Ready
Package; issuance is Automatum's under clause 6.3.

**5.7 Channel Reviews.** OBP will prepare and deliver Channel Reviews at the cadence stated
for the applicable Tier in Schedule 1.

**5.8 Selling Authorizations.** OBP will prepare, and submit to Automatum for issuance,
requests for recurring Selling Authorizations for Recruited Partners, and will track their
status in the PSR. Issuance is Automatum's under clause 6.3.

**5.9 Partner System of Record.** OBP will stand up, configure, host, operate, support and
maintain the PSR for each Engagement in accordance with clause 10 and Schedule 5. The PSR is
supplied within the OBP Service Revenue and is not separately chargeable.

**5.10 Attribution.** OBP will support configuration of AWS Partner Revenue Measurement
attribution for each Registered Client's channel, to the extent that configuration is within
OBP's control. Where configuration requires action by Automatum, the Registered Client or
AWS, OBP's obligation is to specify what is required and to verify the result once done.

**5.11 ACE submissions.** OBP will file partner-sourced opportunities to ACE under the APN
account designated in Schedule 8, and not under any other account. OBP will track
partner-sourced opportunities distinctly from any baseline quota, and will not submit an
opportunity it knows or ought reasonably to know to be inaccurate.

**5.12 Reporting.** OBP will deliver the monthly reporting pack described in Schedule 4 within
five (5) Business Days of each month end, in the Automatum Brand and in a form Automatum can
pass to the Registered Client without alteration.

**5.13 First line of support.** OBP is first line of support for the channel, the deal desk
and the PSR, through the personnel named under clause 9, routed through the single
Automatum-branded intake described in Schedule 3.

**5.14 Compliance with instructions.** OBP will comply with Automatum's reasonable written
instructions relating to the Services, except where compliance would breach this Agreement,
any AWS Program Term or any applicable law. OBP will notify Automatum promptly if it
reasonably believes an instruction would have that effect.

**5.15 Subcontracting.** OBP may subcontract parts of the Services only to subcontractors
approved in writing by Automatum, and remains fully liable for its subcontractors' acts and
omissions as if they were its own. OBP may not subcontract any role named under clause 9.

**6.5 No-worse-than covenant.** Automatum warrants that no Client Agreement for a Registered

  a. a service level stricter than the corresponding service level in Schedule 4;
  b. a service credit percentage or at-risk amount exceeding the caps in Schedule 4;

  d. a metric definition inconsistent with Schedule 4.

  Anything Automatum agrees with a Client beyond this baseline is Automatum's own risk, is

**6.6 No onward commitments.** Automatum will not make any representation, warranty or

**6.7 Relief Events.** Each of the following is a "Relief Event": Automatum's failure to
perform clause 6.1, 6.2 or 6.3; Automatum's loss of its listing, seller of record position or
AWS Partner Network entitlements; a Registered Client's failure to perform an obligation on
which OBP's performance depends; and any matter described in clause 14.3. On a Relief Event,
OBP is excused from the affected obligation and the affected service levels do not run, for so
long as the Relief Event continues, and OBP Service Revenue continues to accrue. OBP will
notify Automatum of a Relief Event promptly on becoming aware of it and will use reasonable
efforts to mitigate its effect.

## 8. Service Levels and Service Credits

**8.1 Service levels.** OBP will meet the service levels in Schedule 4 for the applicable
Tier. Schedule 4 states, for each service level, what starts and stops the clock, the
measurement source, and the division of the measured period between the OBP leg and the
Automatum leg.

**8.2 Split legs.** Because Automatum issues offers and Selling Authorizations as seller of
record, the deal desk and Selling Authorization service levels are measured in two legs. OBP
is responsible only for the OBP leg. A failure of the Automatum leg is not OBP's failure and
does not give rise to a flow-down under clause 8.4. Where a root cause analysis is genuinely
inconclusive as between the legs, responsibility is apportioned equally and either Party may
escalate under clause 26.2.

**8.3 Excused events.** A service level does not run, and no failure occurs, to the extent the
delay is caused by a Relief Event, a Force Majeure Event, an incomplete or non-conforming Deal
Desk Request, a Recruited Partner's failure to complete AWS registration, tax, banking or
service-linked role requirements, a Registered Client's act or omission, or an act or omission
of AWS. A change in OBP's personnel is **not** an excused event and service levels continue to
run through any replacement under clause 9.

**8.4 Flow-down of Service Credits.** Where Automatum is required to give a Registered Client
a Service Credit, Automatum may deduct the amount of that Service Credit from the next monthly
OBP Service Revenue for that Registered Client, but only to the extent that:

  a. the failure was a failure of the OBP leg and is attributable to OBP;
  b. Automatum actually gave the Service Credit to the Client, and did not give it ex gratia,
     as a commercial concession, or in settlement of an unrelated matter, without OBP's prior
     written consent;
  c. Automatum applied every exclusion, cap and excused event available to it under the Client
     Agreement; and
  d. Automatum notified OBP of the Client's claim, with supporting evidence, within ten (10)
     Business Days of receiving it, and gave OBP a reasonable opportunity to comment.

**8.5 Caps on flow-down.** The total amount deductible under clause 8.4 is capped at:

  a. per Registered Client per Engagement Month, the lower of the Service Credit actually
     given and ten percent (10%) of that Client's gross monthly Client Fees, rising to twenty
     percent (20%) in a month in which two or more service levels are missed; and
  b. per Registered Client per Engagement Year, fifteen percent (15%) of the OBP Service
     Revenue for that Client for that Engagement Year.

**8.6 Sole financial remedy.** Subject to clause 8.7, the flow-down in clause 8.4 is
Automatum's sole financial remedy for a failure to meet a service level.

**8.7 Chronic Failure.** "Chronic Failure" means three (3) failures of the same service level
in any rolling three (3) Engagement Months, or six (6) failures across any service levels in
an Engagement Year, in each case for the same Registered Client and attributable to OBP. On a
Chronic Failure, Automatum may require: a root cause analysis within five (5) Business Days; a
written remediation plan within ten (10) Business Days; replacement of the personnel named
under clause 9 for that Engagement; and, if the Chronic Failure is not remedied within thirty
(30) days of the remediation plan, termination of the affected Engagement Order under clause
23.4 with transition assistance at OBP's cost under clause 24.

## 9. Key Personnel

**9.1 Named roles.** For each Engagement, OBP will name in the Engagement Order a Channel
Manager, a Backup Channel Manager and, for a Tier 3 Engagement, a Deal Desk Owner with a
stated coverage window. Each named role has the committed hours and coverage window stated in
Schedule 7.

**9.2 Approval.** Automatum approves initial appointees, such approval not to be unreasonably
withheld, conditioned or delayed, and deemed given if Automatum does not object within five
(5) Business Days of receiving the appointee's details.

**9.3 Minimum tenure.** OBP will not voluntarily remove or reassign a named person for twelve
(12) months or the remainder of the Engagement, whichever is shorter, except where the person
resigns, dies, becomes incapacitated, takes statutory leave, is dismissed for cause, or is
removed at Automatum's request.

**9.4 Replacement.** OBP will notify Automatum within two (2) Business Days of becoming aware
that a named person will cease to perform the role. The Backup Channel Manager assumes the
role immediately. OBP will propose a replacement of equivalent or greater experience within
ten (10) Business Days and will provide a ten (10) Business Day handover overlap at OBP's
cost. Clause 8.3 applies: service levels continue to run throughout.

**9.5 Removal.** Automatum may require OBP to remove a named person from an Engagement for
material underperformance, misconduct, breach of Schedule 3, or repeated service level
failures attributable to that person. Replacement is at OBP's cost. A request under this
clause is a request to remove the person from the Engagement and is not a direction to
dismiss or discipline; OBP remains solely responsible for all employment decisions.

**9.6 No dedication.** Named personnel are assigned on a shared service basis and are not
exclusively dedicated to an Engagement unless the Engagement Order says so.

**9.7 Capacity ratios.** OBP will not exceed the accounts-per-owner ratios in Schedule 7,
which weight Engagements by Tier. Exceeding a ratio is a breach of this Agreement whether or
not a service level has been missed, and Automatum may require a remediation plan under clause
8.7 on that basis alone.

**9.8 Succession.** OBP will maintain a documented succession plan for each named role, will
cross-train the Backup Channel Manager, and will ensure that client and partner knowledge is
recorded in the PSR rather than held only by individuals.

## 10. Partner System of Record

**10.1 Provision.** OBP will provide, configure, host and operate the PSR for each Engagement
in accordance with Schedule 5, within the OBP Service Revenue.

**10.2 Rights warranty.** OBP warrants that it holds all rights necessary to provide and
operate the PSR as contemplated by this Agreement, including the right to present it under the
Automatum Brand and to grant the access and licences in this clause 10, and that the term of
its own licence from the PSR vendor extends beyond the expiry of the longest Engagement Order
then in effect plus the transition period in clause 24.

**10.3 White label configuration.** Each Registered Client's PSR tenant will be configured
under the Automatum Brand in accordance with Schedule 3, with no PSR vendor or OBP branding
visible to the Client or to any Recruited Partner except where the vendor's licence terms make
removal impossible, in which case OBP will notify Automatum before the tenant goes live.

**10.4 Data ownership and permitted use.** Client Data is owned by the Registered Client to
which it relates and is licensed to Automatum under the relevant Client Agreement. OBP may
process Client Data only to deliver the Services for that Registered Client. OBP will not use
Client Data, in identifiable or aggregated form, for benchmarking, for product development,
for its own channel business, or for the benefit of any other client of OBP.

**10.5 Standing data replica.** OBP will export Client Data to storage that Automatum controls,
in the documented machine-readable schema maintained under Schedule 5, daily for transactional
records and monthly as a full snapshot. The Parties will test a restore of the replica
quarterly and reconcile record counts. The Parties acknowledge that source code escrow would
not give Automatum an operable system, because OBP licenses rather than owns the PSR, and that
the standing data replica and the vendor continuity undertaking in clause 10.8 are the primary
continuity protections under this Agreement.

**10.6 Audit record integrity.** OBP will maintain an append-only event log of the events that
constitute the Offer-Issuing Partner count and each other metric in Schedule 4, will retain it
for the Term plus seven (7) years, and will make it available to Automatum, to a Registered
Client and to their respective auditors on reasonable notice. The retention obligation
survives termination.

**10.7 Administrative access.** Automatum will hold administrative access to each Registered
Client's PSR tenant throughout the Engagement.

**10.8 Vendor continuity.** OBP will procure from the PSR vendor, and maintain, a written
undertaking in the form of Schedule 5 Part D under which, on OBP's insolvency, cessation of
business, or termination of this Agreement for OBP's breach, the vendor will contract directly
with Automatum or its nominee for the remainder of the affected Engagement Orders plus the
transition period, at the same or benchmarked rates, with the existing tenant data intact.

## 11. Fees, Invoicing and Payment

**11.1 Collected Client Fees.** "Collected Client Fees" means, for a Registered Client and an
Engagement Month, the Client Fees actually received by Automatum for that month, net of Taxes
and of any payment processing fees, and net of any AWS Marketplace listing fee if and to the
extent clause 11.10 applies.

**11.2 The split.** For each Engagement Year:

  a. "**Automatum Retention**" is nine thousand nine hundred ninety nine dollars ($9,999),
     being Automatum's Enterprise tier, which accrues pro rata across the twelve (12)
     Engagement Months of that Engagement Year at eight hundred thirty three dollars and
     twenty five cents ($833.25) per Engagement Month; and
  b. "**OBP Service Revenue**" is the balance of the Collected Client Fees for that Engagement
     Year after the Automatum Retention, which accrues monthly as set out in Schedule 2.

  Schedule 2 states the resulting figures for each Tier. The Automatum Retention is the same
  amount at every Tier.

**11.3 No front-loading.** The Automatum Retention accrues pro rata under clause 11.2(a) and
is not recovered from the earliest Client payments in priority to the OBP Service Revenue.

**11.4 Invoicing.** OBP will invoice Automatum monthly in arrears for the OBP Service Revenue
accrued in the preceding Engagement Month, together with any amount payable under Schedule 2
for a change in Tier.

**11.5 Payment.** Automatum will pay each undisputed OBP invoice within ten (10) Business Days
of receiving the corresponding Client payment, and in any event within sixty (60) days of the
invoice date whether or not the Client has paid. The sixty (60) day longstop does not apply
where the Client has entered an insolvency process, or where the Client is withholding payment
because of a dispute caused by OBP's performance, in which case only the disputed portion may
be withheld and only until the dispute is resolved.

**11.6 Automatum's collection obligations.** Automatum will invoice each Registered Client
within five (5) Business Days of the due date under the Client Agreement, will pursue
collection diligently, and will provide OBP with a monthly statement showing, for each
Registered Client, the Client Fees invoiced, the amounts collected, the Automatum Retention
accrued and the OBP Service Revenue accrued.

**11.7 No unilateral concessions.** Automatum will not settle, waive, discount or credit any
Client Fees in a way that reduces OBP Service Revenue without OBP's prior written consent. If
it does, OBP Service Revenue is calculated as though the full Client Fees had been collected.

**11.8 Suspension.** OBP may not suspend any client-facing element of the Services for
non-payment unless payment is more than sixty (60) days overdue and OBP has given fifteen (15)
Business Days' written notice to Automatum. OBP's remedy for sustained non-payment is
termination of the affected Engagement Order under clause 23.4 with transition assistance paid
by Automatum. Clause 7.4 applies throughout: OBP will not contact the Registered Client about
a payment dispute.

**11.9 Interest and set-off.** Interest accrues on overdue undisputed amounts at one and
one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.
Neither Party may set off against amounts due except for sums that are liquidated, undisputed
and due.

**11.10 Marketplace transaction of engagement fees.** The Parties record that the engagement
fees are invoiced by Automatum directly to each Client and are not transacted through AWS
Marketplace, so no AWS Marketplace listing fee applies to them, and that the figures in
Schedule 2 depend on that being so. If the Parties later agree to transact engagement fees
through AWS Marketplace, the allocation of the resulting listing fee is a change requiring
agreement under clause 14.5 before that arrangement begins.

**11.11 Taxes.** Each Party is responsible for its own taxes on its own income. Amounts payable
under this Agreement are exclusive of Taxes, which Automatum will pay in addition where
properly chargeable. If any withholding is required by law, the payer will gross up so that
the payee receives the amount it would have received absent the withholding.

**11.12 Audit of billing.** Once per calendar year, OBP may audit Automatum's records relating
to Client Fees invoiced and collected for Registered Clients, on thirty (30) days' notice,
during business hours, at OBP's cost. If the audit shows an understatement of OBP Service
Revenue exceeding five percent (5%) for the period audited, Automatum will bear the cost of
the audit and pay the shortfall with interest under clause 11.9.

# Schedule 1 - Services Description and Tier Matrix

## 1. The Solution

Managed Partner Development is a twelve (12) month engagement under which Automatum builds and
runs a reseller channel on AWS for a Registered Client. Delivery comprises executed partner
agreements and a margin schedule, a live partner portal carrying onboarding, deal registration
and commission tracking, recruitment and enablement on a named cadence, a deal desk answering
registrations, and a monthly report whose headline number is the Offer-Issuing Partner count.

## 2. Tier matrix

| | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Client Fees | $2,500 per month | $5,000 per month | $7,500 per month |
| Net new Recruited Partners (ceiling) | up to 6 | up to 12 | uncapped roster |
| Training and enablement sessions | twice yearly | quarterly | monthly |
| Deal desk turnaround (total) | by close of the next Business Day | 8 Business Hours | 4 Business Hours |
| Channel Reviews | quarterly | monthly | fortnightly |

At every Tier: recurring Selling Authorizations prepared, issued and tracked in AWS
Marketplace; the PSR stood up and operated within the OBP Service Revenue; AWS Partner Revenue
Measurement attribution configured for the channel.

## 3. Partner numbers are a ceiling

The partner numbers are the maximum OBP is obliged to recruit and onboard. Clause 5.3 governs.
OBP does not warrant that any partner is recruited, authorized, issues an offer, transacts or
becomes an Active Partner.

## 4. Tier 3 "uncapped" means no ceiling, not unlimited rate

For a Tier 3 Engagement there is no contractual ceiling on the total number of Recruited
Partners over the Engagement. OBP's obligation is a throughput obligation:

- OBP will process up to the Onboarding Throughput Rate stated in the Engagement Order in new
  partner onboardings per month, and will maintain up to the stated number of concurrent
  onboardings, operated as a first-in-first-out queue with queue position visible in the PSR.
- Requests above the rate are queued. Queuing is not a failure to meet a service level.
- Each Tier 3 Engagement Order states Baseline Assumptions for active partner count and monthly
  onboarding volume. If actual volume exceeds one hundred fifty percent (150%) of Baseline over
  any rolling three (3) months, either Party may invoke clause 14.5, and Automatum will use
  reasonable efforts to obtain an uplift from the Client or a move to a bespoke tier. If the
  Client will not agree, OBP's obligation is capped at Baseline plus fair use and Automatum
  carries the difference against the Client.
- Above the partner record threshold stated in the Engagement Order, incremental PSR licence
  cost is passed through at cost under clause 14.5, unless OBP has warranted in the Engagement
  Order that it holds an unlimited-partner licence.

## 5. Services at every Tier

**5.1 Recruitment.** Identify, qualify, approach, recruit and onboard Recruited Partners
against the ideal partner profile agreed in the Engagement Order.

**5.2 Partner due diligence.** Before onboarding, screen each candidate against: sanctions and
denied party lists; adverse media; beneficial ownership, including whether any owner or
controller is a government official; AWS Partner Network standing; and financial standing.
Clause 19.4 governs the consequences.

**5.3 Partner contracting.** Draft, issue and administer the partner agreement and margin
schedule, executed between the Registered Client, or Automatum as its agent, and the partner.
Clause 5.4 governs. OBP is never a principal.

**5.4 Onboarding.** Guide each partner through AWS registration, tax and banking details, and
service-linked role creation. Clause 8.3 governs delay caused by a partner's failure to
complete these, which is outside OBP's control.

**5.5 Enablement and training.** Deliver sessions at the Tier cadence, covering the Client's
product, the offer mechanics, deal registration and the margin schedule.

**5.6 Deal desk.** Receive Deal Desk Requests and deliver an Issuance-Ready Package to
Automatum within the OBP leg in Schedule 4.

**5.7 Selling Authorizations.** Prepare and submit recurring Selling Authorization requests to
Automatum and track their status in the PSR.

**5.8 Channel Reviews.** Deliver reviews at the Tier cadence covering pipeline, partner
performance, the headline metric and remediation actions.

**5.9 PSR.** Stand up, configure, host, operate, support and maintain the PSR under clause 10
and Schedule 5.

**5.10 Attribution.** Support configuration of AWS Partner Revenue Measurement attribution as
described in clause 5.10 of this Agreement.

**5.11 ACE.** File partner-sourced opportunities under the account in Schedule 8, tracked
distinctly from any baseline quota.

**5.12 Reporting.** Deliver the monthly reporting pack in Schedule 4.

## 6. Partner milestones are targets, not warranties

The Parties record the programme target that a Recruited Partner achieves its first
Transaction by the end of the first quarter following its partner agreement signature date.
This is a target supported by a best efforts obligation and a reporting duty. It is not a
warranty, because partner timelines depend on the partner completing AWS registration, tax,
banking and service-linked role requirements, which is outside the control of OBP, Automatum
and the Registered Client. Clause 8.3 stops the clock while a partner is pending those
requirements.

## 7. Active Partner measurement

A Recruited Partner is an Active Partner only if it has transacted at least once in the
preceding three hundred sixty five (365) days. Because an Engagement Year is twelve (12)
months, a partner recruited late in an Engagement cannot reach Active Partner status within
that Engagement. Accordingly:

- Active Partner counts are measured on a trailing basis and are also measured at a post-term
  measurement date falling three (3) months after the end of the Engagement Year;
- Recruited Partners onboarded in the final ninety (90) days of an Engagement Year are excluded
  from any in-term ratio expressed by reference to Active Partners; and
- no fee, credit or commitment under this Agreement is calculated by reference to an Active
  Partner count for a period in which the partner could not arithmetically have qualified.

## 8. Out of scope

Anything not described in this Schedule 1, including bespoke integration work, partner
marketing spend, channel incentive funding, legal advice, and services for a Client's direct
customers, is out of scope and chargeable under clause 14.5 at the rate card in Schedule 2.

# Schedule 4 - Service Levels, Measurement and Credits

## 1. Business Hours

Business Hours are 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Eastern Time on a Business Day, unless the
Engagement Order states a different coverage window for a Tier 3 Deal Desk Owner.

## 2. Deal desk turnaround

A Deal Desk Request is "Complete" when it contains every item in the request form in the
Engagement Order. The clock starts when a Complete Deal Desk Request is received through the
intake channel, or at the start of the next Business Hour if received outside Business Hours.
The clock stops when the responsible Party delivers its output.

The total turnaround is split into two legs, because Automatum issues offers as seller of
record and OBP cannot control issuance. An "Issuance-Ready Package" is a completed CPPO
structure, pricing, partner and end customer details and supporting documentation in the form
in the Engagement Order.

| Tier | Total turnaround | OBP leg: Complete request to Issuance-Ready Package | Automatum leg: package received to offer issued |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | By close of the next Business Day after receipt | 6 Business Hours | 3 Business Hours |
| 2 | 8 Business Hours | 5 Business Hours | 3 Business Hours |
| 3 | 4 Business Hours | 2.5 Business Hours | 1.5 Business Hours |

The Tier 1 and Tier 2 turnarounds are deliberately defined so that they differ. Tier 1 is
satisfied if the offer is issued by close of the Business Day following the Business Day of
receipt, which permits up to approximately sixteen (16) Business Hours elapsed. Tier 2 is a
fixed eight (8) Business Hours from receipt. Without this paragraph the two Tiers would sell
the same turnaround, because a Business Day and eight Business Hours are otherwise the same
period.

Clause 8.2 governs attribution between the legs.

## 5. Metric dictionary

**5.1 Primary metric - Offer-Issuing Partners.** This is the metric the feasibility study
calls "partners issuing offers". It is the number of distinct Recruited Partners

the reporting month, read from the PSR offer record and reconciled against AWS Marketplace
seller reporting. This metric applies if and only if the Marketplace authorization dependency
in clause 14.2 is confirmed as available by the longstop date in Schedule 8.

**5.2 Fallback metric - Partner-Attributed Transactions.** The number of Transactions issued by
Automatum against a Selling Authorization or private offer attributed to a named Recruited
Partner through AWS Partner Revenue Measurement and a matching opportunity identifier, during
the reporting month, read from the PSR and reconciled against AWS Marketplace seller
reporting. This metric applies automatically under clause 14.2(c) if the dependency is not
confirmed by the longstop date.

**5.3 Both metrics are defined now.** The Parties have defined the fallback metric at the time
of contracting rather than agreeing to agree it later, because if it were left open it would
fall to be negotiated at the moment the dependency resolved unfavourably and the Tier table
had already been published.

**5.4 Supporting measures.** The monthly reporting pack also reports: Recruited Partners
onboarded in the month and cumulatively; partners pending AWS prerequisites; Active Partners

Deal Desk Requests and turnaround performance by leg; Selling Authorizations requested and
issued; and channel revenue attributed.

Automatum carries client credit risk, service level liability, collection risk and the client
relationship. **Open commercial item: whether the Retention is a margin or a pass-through of
Automatum's own cost**, and whether a prime management fee should sit on top of it. The
feasibility study does not say. This Agreement implements the study's figures as written.

**3.5 Stress testing at the wrong end.** PSR licensing was stress-tested against Tier 1, the
cheapest and most predictable case, while Tier 3 carries an uncapped roster. If the PSR is
priced per partner or per seat, Tier 3 is where the model breaks. Schedule 5 Part A records
this as a question to close, and Schedule 1 paragraph 4 provides the pass-through if the
answer is unfavourable.

**3.6 AWS is not committed.** The feasibility study's reference to five ACE opportunity
submissions is recorded as a programme assumption only. It is not a commitment by AWS, by
Automatum or by OBP, and clause 13.3 governs.

**3.7 Partner AWS prerequisites.** Recruited Partner timelines depend on the partner completing
AWS registration, tax, banking and service-linked role requirements, which is outside the
control of either Party and of the Registered Client. Clause 8.3 and Schedule 1 paragraph 6
govern.
