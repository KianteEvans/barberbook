**Managed Partner Development**

**[to supply: Automatum legal entity name]**, a [to supply: entity type] organized under the

## Recitals

**A.** Automatum publishes software through AWS Marketplace, holds the seller of record
position for its listing, and issues private offers and Selling Authorizations under it. OBVG
operates a managed channel function and licenses and operates a partner system of record.

**B.** Managed Partner Development is OBVG's service. OBVG developed it and it forms part of
the OBVG Materials. Under it a software vendor with a live AWS Marketplace listing engages a
single provider to build and run its reseller channel on AWS. Automatum sells it under the
Automatum Brand on the terms of this Agreement.

**C.** Automatum contracts with, invoices and faces each client. OBVG performs the channel
function as Automatum's subcontractor under Automatum's brand and is not presented to the
client as a separate provider. The arrangement is non-exclusive.

The Parties agree as follows.

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

### 1.1 Definitions

"**Active Partner**" means a Recruited Partner that has completed at least one Transaction in
the preceding 365 days, whatever its onboarding status.

"**ACE**" means the AWS Partner Central APN Customer Engagements program. "**AWS**" means
Amazon Web Services, Inc. and its affiliates. "**AWS Marketplace**" means the AWS Marketplace
digital catalog. "**CPPO**" means a Channel Partner Private Offer made through AWS
Marketplace.

"**AWS Program Terms**" means the AWS Partner Network terms, the AWS Marketplace Seller Terms
and Conditions, the AWS Customer Agreement, the ACE program terms, and any other AWS terms,
policies or program rules applicable to a Party, as amended by AWS from time to time.

"**Automatum Brand**" means the Automatum names, logos, marks, domains, email domains,
templates and other brand assets Automatum makes available to OBVG.

"**Business Day**" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in the State of
New York. "**Business Hours**" means 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Eastern Time on a Business Day,
unless an Engagement Order states a different coverage window.

"**Channel Review**" means a governance session with a Registered Client covering partner
pipeline, partner performance, the headline metric and remediation, at the Tier cadence in
Schedule 1.

"**Client**" means a software vendor that contracts with Automatum for the Solution.
"**Client Agreement**" means the agreement under which Automatum supplies the Solution to a
Client. "**Client Fees**" means the fees payable by a Registered Client under its Client
Agreement for the Solution, excluding Taxes and fees for services outside the Solution.

"**Client Data**" means all data relating to a Registered Client, its Recruited Partners and
its channel created, collected, processed or stored in delivering the Services, including
partner records, deal registrations, commission records, offer records and reporting outputs.

"**Client Registry**" means the register of Registered Clients maintained under clause 4.

"**Client Source**" means whether a Registered Client is OBVG-Sourced, Automatum-Sourced, or
Joint or ACE-Sourced, determined under clause 4.7. "**Client Status**" means whether a
Registered Client is a Net New Client or an Existing Automatum Client, determined under clause
4.7.

"**Collected Client Fees**" means, for a Registered Client and an Engagement Month, the Client
Fees actually received by Automatum for that month, net of Taxes, payment processing fees, and
any AWS Marketplace listing fee to the extent clause 11.9 applies.

"**Deal Desk Request**" means a request by or for a Recruited Partner or Registered Client to
structure, price, register or progress a CPPO, submitted through the intake channel in the
form specified in the Engagement Order.

"**Engagement**" means supply of the Solution to one Registered Client under one Engagement
Order. "**Engagement Month**" means a calendar month, or part of one, during which an
Engagement is in effect. "**Engagement Year**" means each 12 consecutive months from the start
date in an Engagement Order.

"**Engagement Order**" means an order in the form of Schedule 6, executed by both Parties,
adding a Client to the Client Registry and engaging OBVG for that Client.

"**Existing Automatum Client**" means a Client that, at the date its Engagement Order is
executed or in the 90 days before, subscribes or subscribed to Automatum's Enterprise tier or
any other paid Automatum subscription. "**Net New Client**" means any other Client.

"**Issuance-Ready Package**" means a completed CPPO structure, pricing, partner and end
customer details and supporting documentation, in the form stated in the Engagement Order,
delivered by OBVG so that Automatum can issue the offer.

"**Losses**" means losses, liabilities, damages, judgments, settlements, fines, penalties,
costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**OBVG Materials**" means the methodologies, playbooks, templates, partner agreement and
margin schedule forms, curricula, enablement content, processes and know-how OBVG owns or
licenses and uses in delivering the Services, together with the PSR.

"**OBVG Service Revenue**" means OBVG's share of Service Revenue under clause 11.2.

"**Offer-Issuing Partner**" means, for a Registered Client and a measurement period, a
Recruited Partner that issued at least one CPPO against that Client's listing in that period,
as recorded in the PSR and determined under Schedule 4.

"**Partner System of Record**" or "**PSR**" means the partner management platform licensed and
operated by OBVG under clause 10 and Schedule 5, providing partner portal, onboarding, deal
registration and commission tracking, with HubSpot and Salesforce synchronization.

"**Personal Data**" means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person

"**Platform Revenue**" means $9,999 per Engagement Year, being the price of Automatum's

"**Recruited Partner**" means a reseller, systems integrator, managed service provider or

"**Registered Client**" means a Client entered in the Client Registry under clause 4 for which

"**Selling Authorization**" means an authorization issued by Automatum through AWS Marketplace

## 2. Structure and Order of Precedence

**2.1** This Agreement consists of these clauses, the Schedules, and each Engagement Order.
An Engagement Order incorporates this Agreement by reference and takes effect when signed by
both Parties.

**2.2** On conflict, the following prevails in descending order: an Engagement Order, but only
for its own Engagement and only where it expressly identifies the provision it varies; these
clauses; the Schedules.

**2.3** A Client Agreement is not part of this Agreement, does not bind OBVG, and cannot vary
it. Clause 6.5 governs flow-down.

## 3. Appointment

**3.1 Appointment.** Automatum appoints OBVG as a delivery subcontractor to perform the
Services for Registered Clients under the Automatum Brand, and OBVG accepts.

**3.2 Non-exclusive.** The appointment is non-exclusive both ways. Automatum may perform the
Services itself or appoint others for any client, and OBVG may provide the same or similar
services to anyone, including another AWS Marketplace seller. This is subject only to clause
15, which operates solely in respect of Registered Clients and Recruited Partners.

**3.3 No volume commitment.** Automatum commits to no minimum number of Engagements,
Registered Clients or Client Fees, and OBVG has not relied on any forecast, projection or
business case exchanged before the Effective Date.

**3.4 No agency.** OBVG is an independent contractor, not Automatum's agent, employee, partner
or joint venturer, and may not bind Automatum except as Automatum authorizes in writing for a
specific matter.

**3.5 Prime responsibility.** Automatum is the prime contractor and is responsible to each
Registered Client for the Solution as a whole. As between the Parties, OBVG is responsible for
the Services and for its personnel and subcontractors.

## 4. Client Registry and Engagement Orders

**4.1 Purpose.** The Client Registry is the sole record of which Clients are subject to this
Agreement. A Client is within scope only once entered in it.

**4.2 Adding a Client.** A Client is added when the Parties execute an Engagement Order.
Automatum maintains the Registry and provides a current copy with each monthly statement under
clause 11.5 and on request.

**4.3 Engagement Order contents.** Each Engagement Order states at least: the Client's legal
name; the Tier; the Client Status and Client Source; the Engagement start and Engagement Year
dates; the Client Fees and the resulting OBVG Service Revenue; the personnel named under clause
9; the PSR seat allocation; and any variation permitted by clause 2.2.

**4.4 Prior relationships.** Before an Engagement Order is executed, OBVG may notify Automatum
in writing, with reasonable evidence, of a pre-existing relationship with the proposed Client
or a named partner of it. A relationship so notified is a "Prior Relationship" and is carved
out of clause 15.2. A relationship not notified before execution is not a Prior Relationship.

**4.5 Refusal.** OBVG may decline a proposed Engagement Order within 5 Business Days, acting
reasonably, including for lack of capacity at the proposed Tier, because the proposed Client
competes with an existing OBVG client, or because the proposed Client's existing Client
Agreement does not meet clause 6.5. Declining is not a breach, and Automatum may then serve
that Client itself or through another provider.

**4.6 Removal.** A Client remains in the Registry while its Engagement is in effect and for
the period in clause 15.2, then is marked closed. Closure does not affect clause 23.6.

**4.7 Client Status and Client Source.** Each Engagement Order records two classifications,
fixed at execution and not revisited during that Engagement Year:

  a. **Client Status** - Net New Client or Existing Automatum Client. Automatum will disclose
     before execution whether the proposed Client holds or held any paid Automatum
     subscription in the preceding 90 days, with reasonable evidence on request. Client Status
     determines the division of Service Revenue under clause 11.2.
  b. **Client Source** - OBVG-Sourced, Automatum-Sourced, or Joint or ACE-Sourced, by which
     Party first made documented contact with the Client about the Solution, and Joint or
     ACE-Sourced where the opportunity came through ACE or neither Party can show first
     contact.

  Client Status is re-determined on a renewal Engagement Order. A dispute about either
  classification is escalated under clause 26.2.

## 5. OBVG Delivery Obligations

**5.1 The Services.** For each Engagement OBVG will, at the applicable Tier and to the service
levels in Schedule 4, using suitably skilled personnel and with reasonable skill and care:

  a. identify, qualify, recruit and onboard Recruited Partners against the ideal partner
     profile in the Engagement Order, up to the number stated for that Tier, screening each
     candidate before onboarding against sanctions and denied party lists, adverse media,
     beneficial ownership including whether any owner or controller is a government official,
     AWS Partner Network standing, and financial standing;
  b. draft, issue and administer the partner agreement and margin schedule;
  c. guide each partner through AWS registration, tax and banking details and service-linked
     role creation;
  d. deliver enablement and training sessions at the Tier cadence, covering the Client's
     product, offer mechanics, deal registration and the margin schedule;
  e. operate the CPPO deal desk, delivering an Issuance-Ready Package to Automatum within the
     OBVG leg in Schedule 4;
  f. prepare and submit to Automatum requests for recurring Selling Authorizations, and track
     their status in the PSR;
  g. deliver Channel Reviews at the Tier cadence;
  h. stand up, configure, host, operate, support and maintain the PSR under clause 10 and
     Schedule 5, within the OBVG Service Revenue and not separately chargeable;
  i. support configuration of AWS Partner Revenue Measurement attribution so far as within
     OBVG's control, and where action is required of Automatum, the Client or AWS, specify what
     is required and verify the result;
  j. file partner-sourced opportunities to ACE under the APN account in Schedule 8 and no
     other, tracked distinctly from any baseline quota; and
  k. deliver the monthly reporting pack in Schedule 4 within 5 Business Days of month end, in
     the Automatum Brand and in a form Automatum can pass to the Client unaltered.

**5.2 Recruitment is a ceiling, not a minimum.** The partner numbers in Schedule 1 are the
maximum OBVG must recruit and onboard. OBVG does not warrant, and need not procure, that any
minimum number of partners is recruited, onboarded, authorized, transacts or becomes an Active
Partner, and Automatum will not represent otherwise. This clause prevails over any contrary
reading of Schedule 1.

**5.3 Partner contracting.** Each partner agreement is executed between the Registered Client,
or Automatum as its agent, and the Recruited Partner. **OBVG is not a party to any partner
agreement as principal** and will not enter into one in its own name. OBVG administers them as
agent under the authority in the Engagement Order and no wider.

**5.4 Deal desk boundary.** OBVG's deal desk obligation ends on delivery of the Issuance-Ready
Package. Issuance is Automatum's under clause 6.3.

**5.5 First line of support.** OBVG is first line for the channel, the deal desk and the PSR,
through the personnel named under clause 9, routed through the single Automatum-branded intake
in Schedule 3.

**5.6 Instructions.** OBVG will follow Automatum's reasonable written instructions relating to
the Services, except where doing so would breach this Agreement, an AWS Program Term or any
law, and will notify Automatum promptly if it believes an instruction would.

**5.7 Subcontracting.** OBVG may subcontract only to subcontractors Automatum approves in
writing, remains fully liable for them, and may not subcontract any role named under clause 9.

## 8. Service Levels and Service Credits

**8.1 Service levels.** OBVG will meet the service levels in Schedule 4, which state for each
what starts and stops the clock, the measurement source, and the division between the OBVG leg
and the Automatum leg.

**8.2 Split legs.** Because Automatum issues offers and Selling Authorizations as seller of
record, the deal desk and Selling Authorization service levels are measured in two legs. OBVG
is responsible only for the OBVG leg, and a failure of the Automatum leg gives rise to no
flow-down under clause 8.4. Where root cause analysis is genuinely inconclusive between the
legs, responsibility is apportioned equally and either Party may escalate under clause 26.2.

**8.3 Excused events.** A service level does not run, and no failure occurs, to the extent
delay is caused by a Relief Event, a Force Majeure Event, an incomplete or non-conforming Deal
Desk Request, a Recruited Partner's failure to complete AWS registration, tax, banking or
service-linked role requirements, a Registered Client's act or omission, or an act or omission
of AWS. A change in OBVG's personnel is **not** an excused event and service levels continue to
run through any replacement under clause 9.

**8.4 Flow-down of Service Credits.** Where Automatum must give a Registered Client a Service
Credit, it may deduct that amount from the next monthly OBVG Service Revenue for that Client,
but only where the failure was of the OBVG leg and attributable to OBVG; Automatum actually gave
the credit and did not give it ex gratia, as a concession, or in settlement of an unrelated
matter without OBVG's prior written consent; Automatum applied every exclusion, cap and excused
event available to it; and Automatum notified OBVG of the claim with supporting evidence within
10 Business Days of receiving it and gave OBVG a reasonable opportunity to comment.

**8.5 Caps on flow-down.** Deductions under clause 8.4 are capped, per Registered Client, at
the lower of the credit actually given and 10% of that Client's gross monthly Client Fees per
Engagement Month, rising to 20% in a month in which two or more service levels are missed; and
at 15% of that Client's OBVG Service Revenue per Engagement Year.

**8.6 Sole financial remedy.** Subject to clause 8.7, clause 8.4 is Automatum's sole financial
remedy for a failure to meet a service level.

**8.7 Chronic Failure.** "Chronic Failure" means 3 failures of the same service level in any
rolling 3 Engagement Months, or 6 failures across any service levels in an Engagement Year,
for the same Registered Client and attributable to OBVG. Automatum may then require a root
cause analysis within 5 Business Days, a written remediation plan within 10 Business Days, and
replacement of the personnel named under clause 9 for that Engagement; and if the Chronic
Failure is not remedied within 30 days of the remediation plan, may terminate the affected
Engagement Order under clause 23.4 with transition assistance at OBVG's cost.

## 9. Key Personnel

**9.1 Named roles.** Each Engagement Order names a Channel Manager, a Backup Channel Manager
and, at Tier 3, a Deal Desk Owner with a stated coverage window, each with the committed hours
and coverage window in Schedule 7.

**9.2 Approval.** Automatum approves initial appointees, not to be unreasonably withheld,
conditioned or delayed, and deemed given if Automatum does not object within 5 Business Days
of receiving the appointee's details.

**9.3 Minimum tenure.** OBVG will not voluntarily remove or reassign a named person for 12
months or the remainder of the Engagement, whichever is shorter, unless the person resigns,
dies, becomes incapacitated, takes statutory leave, is dismissed for cause, or is removed at
Automatum's request.

**9.4 Replacement.** OBVG will notify Automatum within 2 Business Days of becoming aware that a
named person will cease in the role. The Backup Channel Manager assumes it immediately. OBVG
will propose a replacement of equivalent or greater experience within 10 Business Days and
provide a 10 Business Day handover overlap at its own cost. Clause 8.3 applies throughout.

**9.5 Removal.** Automatum may require removal of a named person from an Engagement for
material underperformance, misconduct, breach of Schedule 3, or repeated attributable service
level failures, and replacement is at OBVG's cost. This is a request to remove the person from
the Engagement, not a direction to dismiss or discipline, and OBVG remains solely responsible
for all employment decisions.

**9.6 No dedication.** Named personnel are assigned on a shared service basis and are not
exclusively dedicated unless the Engagement Order says so.

**9.7 Capacity ratios.** OBVG will not exceed the accounts-per-owner ratios in Schedule 7.
Exceeding a ratio is a breach whether or not a service level has been missed, and Automatum
may require a remediation plan under clause 8.7 on that basis alone.

**9.8 Succession.** OBVG will maintain a documented succession plan for each named role,
cross-train the Backup Channel Manager, and ensure client and partner knowledge is recorded in
the PSR rather than held only by individuals.

**10.2 White label configuration.** Each Registered Client's tenant is configured under the

**10.5 Audit record integrity.** OBVG will maintain an append-only event log of the events

**10.6 Administrative access.** Automatum holds administrative access to each Registered
Client's tenant throughout the Engagement.

**10.7 Vendor continuity.** OBVG will procure and maintain from the PSR vendor a written
undertaking in the form of Schedule 5 Part D, under which on OBVG's insolvency, cessation of

condition to OBVG's first Engagement Order. If the vendor will not give it, OBVG will notify

or require an alternative PSR.

**10.8 Step-in.** Automatum may step in on a Chronic Failure, an insolvency event affecting

compliance event. OBVG will then grant Automatum and any nominee access, credentials and
administrative rights to the PSR and a royalty-free licence to the OBVG Materials for the
remainder of the affected Engagement Orders plus the transition period. Step-in does not
relieve OBVG of liability for any earlier period. Automatum will step out on 30 days' notice

**10.9 Insolvency.** The rights and licences under clauses 10.7 and 10.8 are licences of
intellectual property for the purposes of section 365(n) of the United States Bankruptcy Code,
and Automatum may elect to retain its rights under that section. Neither Party will assert
that those rights terminate automatically on the other's insolvency.

## 11. Fees, Invoicing and Payment

**11.1 The split.** For each Engagement Year the Collected Client Fees divide into Platform
Revenue and Service Revenue. Platform Revenue is $9,999 and belongs to Automatum whatever the
Client Status. Service Revenue divides by Client Status:

  a. **Net New Client** - the whole of the Service Revenue is retained by OBVG, and Automatum
     takes no share of it.
  b. **Existing Automatum Client** - the Client already pays the Platform Revenue under its
     existing subscription, which continues unchanged, and the Service Revenue is divided 75%
     to OBVG and 25% to Automatum in consideration of the Client relationship Automatum brings
     to the Engagement.

  Client Fees are the same at a given Tier whatever the Client Status. Schedule 2 states the
  figures and the rounding rule.

**11.2 Accrual.** Platform Revenue accrues pro rata across the 12 Engagement Months of an
Engagement Year at $833.25 per Engagement Month, and is not recovered from the earliest Client
payments in priority to OBVG Service Revenue. Each Party's share of Service Revenue accrues
monthly at one twelfth of its annual share.

**11.3 Invoicing.** OBVG will invoice Automatum monthly in arrears for the OBVG Service Revenue
accrued in the preceding Engagement Month, with any amount payable under Schedule 2 for a
change in Tier.

**11.4 Payment.** Automatum will pay each undisputed invoice within 10 Business Days of
receiving the corresponding Client payment, and in any event within 60 days of the invoice
date whether or not the Client has paid. The 60 day longstop does not apply where the Client
has entered an insolvency process, or is withholding payment because of a dispute caused by
OBVG's performance, in which case only the disputed portion may be withheld and only until
resolved.

**11.5 Automatum's collection obligations.** Automatum will invoice each Registered Client
within 5 Business Days of the due date under the Client Agreement, pursue collection
diligently, and give OBVG a monthly statement showing for each Registered Client the Client
Status, Client Fees invoiced, amounts collected, Platform Revenue accrued and each Party's
share of Service Revenue accrued.

**11.6 No unilateral concessions.** Automatum will not settle, waive, discount or credit
Client Fees in a way that reduces OBVG Service Revenue without OBVG's prior written consent. If
it does, OBVG Service Revenue is calculated as though the full Client Fees had been collected.

**11.7 Suspension.** OBVG may not suspend any client-facing element of the Services for
non-payment unless payment is more than 60 days overdue and OBVG has given 15 Business Days'
written notice to Automatum. OBVG's remedy for sustained non-payment is termination of the
affected Engagement Order under clause 23.4 with transition assistance paid by Automatum.
Clause 7.4 applies throughout.

# Schedule 1 - Services Description and Tier Matrix

## 1. The Solution

Managed Partner Development is a 12 month engagement under which Automatum builds and runs a
reseller channel on AWS for a Registered Client. Delivery is executed partner agreements and a
margin schedule, a live partner portal carrying onboarding, deal registration and commission
tracking, recruitment and enablement on a named cadence, a deal desk answering registrations,
and a monthly report whose headline number is the Offer-Issuing Partner count. Clause 5.1
states what OBVG performs; this Schedule states what varies by Tier.

## 2. Tier matrix

| | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Client Fees | $2,500 per month | $5,000 per month | $7,500 per month |
| Net new Recruited Partners (ceiling) | up to 6 | up to 12 | uncapped roster |
| Training and enablement sessions | twice yearly | quarterly | monthly |
| Deal desk turnaround (total) | by close of the next Business Day | 8 Business Hours | 4 Business Hours |
| Channel Reviews | quarterly | monthly | fortnightly |

Everything else in clause 5.1 applies at every Tier. Clause 5.2 governs the partner numbers,
which are a ceiling and not a minimum.

## 3. Tier 3: uncapped roster, not unlimited rate

There is no contractual ceiling on the total number of Recruited Partners over a Tier 3
Engagement. OBVG's obligation is throughput:

- OBVG will process up to the Onboarding Throughput Rate in the Engagement Order per month and
  maintain up to the stated number of concurrent onboardings, as a first-in-first-out queue
  with queue position visible in the PSR. Requests above the rate are queued, and queuing is
  not a service level failure.
- Each Tier 3 Engagement Order states Baseline Assumptions for active partner count and
  monthly onboarding volume. If actual volume exceeds 150% of Baseline over any rolling 3
  months, either Party may invoke clause 14.5 and Automatum will use reasonable efforts to
  obtain an uplift or a move to a bespoke tier. If the Client will not agree, OBVG's obligation
  is capped at Baseline plus fair use and Automatum carries the difference against the Client.
- Above the partner record threshold in the Engagement Order, incremental PSR licence cost
  passes through at cost under clause 14.5, unless OBVG has warranted in the Engagement Order
  that it holds an unlimited-partner licence.

## 4. Partner milestones are targets, not warranties

The programme target is that a Recruited Partner achieves its first Transaction by the end of
the first quarter following its partner agreement signature date. This is a target supported
by a best efforts obligation and a reporting duty, not a warranty, because partner timelines
depend on the partner completing AWS registration, tax, banking and service-linked role
requirements, which is outside the control of OBVG, Automatum and the Registered Client. Clause
8.3 stops the clock while a partner is pending those requirements.

## 5. Active Partner measurement

An Engagement Year is 12 months and an Active Partner must have transacted within 365 days, so
a partner recruited late in an Engagement cannot reach Active Partner status within it.
Accordingly Active Partner counts are measured on a trailing basis and again at a post-term
measurement date 3 months after the end of the Engagement Year; Recruited Partners onboarded
in the final 90 days of an Engagement Year are excluded from any in-term ratio expressed by
reference to Active Partners; and no fee, credit or commitment is calculated by reference to
an Active Partner count for a period in which the partner could not arithmetically have
qualified.

## 6. Out of scope

Anything not described in clause 5.1 or this Schedule, including bespoke integration work,
partner marketing spend, channel incentive funding, legal advice, and services for a Client's
direct customers, is chargeable under clause 14.5 at the Schedule 2 rate card.

# Schedule 4 - Service Levels, Measurement and Credits

## 1. Deal desk turnaround

A Deal Desk Request is "Complete" when it contains every item in the request form in the
Engagement Order. The clock starts when a Complete request is received through the intake
channel, or at the start of the next Business Hour if received outside Business Hours, and
stops when the responsible Party delivers its output. Turnaround splits into two legs, because
Automatum issues offers as seller of record and OBVG cannot control issuance.

| Tier | Total turnaround | OBVG leg: Complete request to Issuance-Ready Package | Automatum leg: package to offer issued |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | By close of the next Business Day after receipt | 6 Business Hours | 3 Business Hours |
| 2 | 8 Business Hours | 5 Business Hours | 3 Business Hours |
| 3 | 4 Business Hours | 2.5 Business Hours | 1.5 Business Hours |

Tier 1 is satisfied if the offer is issued by close of the Business Day following the Business
Day of receipt, permitting up to approximately 16 Business Hours elapsed; Tier 2 is a fixed 8
Business Hours from receipt. The two are defined separately because a Business Day and 8
Business Hours would otherwise be the same period. Clause 8.2 governs attribution between legs.

## 2. Selling Authorizations

At every Tier: OBVG leg, request to submission to Automatum, 2 Business Days; Automatum leg,
submission to issuance, 2 Business Days.

## 3. Cadence commitments

Training and enablement sessions and Channel Reviews run at the Tier cadences in the Schedule
1 tier matrix, and the monthly reporting pack is due within 5 Business Days of month end at
every Tier. A cadence commitment is met if the session or review is offered on at least two
proposed dates within the period and is held, or is not held because of the Registered
Client's unavailability.

## 4. Metric dictionary

**4.1 Primary metric - Offer-Issuing Partners.** The number of distinct Recruited Partners
that created at least one private offer or CPPO against the Registered Client's product during the reporting month, read
from the PSR offer record and reconciled against AWS Marketplace seller reporting. It applies
only if the dependency in clause 14.2 is confirmed available by the longstop date in Schedule
8.

**4.2 Fallback metric - Partner-Attributed Transactions.** The number of Transactions issued
by Automatum against a Selling Authorization or private offer attributed to a named Recruited
Partner through AWS Partner Revenue Measurement and a matching opportunity identifier during
the reporting month, read from the PSR and reconciled against AWS Marketplace seller
reporting. It applies automatically under clause 14.2 if the dependency is not confirmed by
the longstop date.

**4.3 Supporting measures.** The monthly reporting pack also reports Recruited Partners
onboarded in the month and cumulatively; partners pending AWS prerequisites; Active Partners
measured under Schedule 1 paragraph 5; deal registrations received, approved and declined;
Deal Desk Requests and turnaround performance by leg; Selling Authorizations requested and
issued; and channel revenue attributed.

## 5. Support model

| Matter | First line | Second line |
|---|---|---|
| Channel, partner queries, enablement | OBVG | Automatum |
| Deal desk and deal registration | OBVG | Automatum |
| Partner System of Record | OBVG | PSR vendor via OBVG |
| Listing, offer issuance, Selling Authorizations | Automatum | AWS via Automatum |

All contact arrives through the single Automatum-branded intake under Schedule 3 paragraph
2.1. OBVG triages within 2 Business Hours and routes to the correct first line. A misrouted

| Priority | Definition | Response | Updates |

| P2 | A partner is blocked on a live deal | 4 Business Hours | daily |

| P4 | Request for information or change | 3 Business Days | weekly |

## 6. Credits

Clause 8.4 governs flow-down, clause 8.5 the caps and clause 8.7 Chronic Failure. A Service

on which the Client Agreement computes it. There is no earn-back.
