Managed Partner Development

Build and run your AWS Marketplace reseller channel.

A 12-month engagement that recruits resellers for your listing, contracts them, enables them and runs the deal desk that gets offers issued. You keep the listing, the seller of record position and the customer relationship. We run the channel behind it.

For software vendors with a live AWS Marketplace listing.

What you get

Every tier includes all of the following.

Partner recruitment and onboarding

We identify, qualify, recruit and onboard resellers against the ideal partner profile we agree with you. Every candidate is screened before onboarding against sanctions and denied party lists, adverse media, beneficial ownership including whether any owner or controller is a government official, AWS Partner Network standing, and financial standing.

Partner contracting

We draft, issue and administer the partner agreement and the margin schedule. The agreement is between you and the partner; we run the paperwork.

AWS readiness

We guide each partner through AWS registration, tax and banking details, and service-linked role creation.

A live partner portal

Onboarding workflow, deal registration with approval and decline history, and commission tracking against the margin schedule, synchronized with HubSpot or Salesforce as you specify.

Enablement and training

Sessions at your tier cadence covering your product, offer mechanics, deal registration and the margin schedule.

A CPPO deal desk

Partner deal registrations answered to a published turnaround, with every private offer and CPPO structured and priced ready for issue.

Selling Authorizations

Prepared, submitted and tracked to issue.

Opportunity hygiene

Partner-sourced opportunities filed to ACE, tracked distinctly, with AWS Partner Revenue Measurement attribution configured and verified.

A monthly report

Delivered within 5 business days of month end, in your brand, in a form you can pass on unaltered.

Headline number
Offer-Issuing Partners
