# Managed Partner Development

**Build and run your AWS Marketplace reseller channel**

A 12-month engagement that recruits resellers for your listing, contracts them, enables them
and runs the deal desk that gets offers issued. You keep the listing, the seller of record
position and the customer relationship. We run the channel behind it.

For software vendors with a live AWS Marketplace listing.

## What you get

Every tier includes all of the following.

**Partner recruitment and onboarding.** We identify, qualify, recruit and onboard resellers
against the ideal partner profile we agree with you. Every candidate is screened before
onboarding against sanctions and denied party lists, adverse media, beneficial ownership
including whether any owner or controller is a government official, AWS Partner Network
standing, and financial standing.

**Partner contracting.** We draft, issue and administer the partner agreement and the margin
schedule. The agreement is between you and the partner; we run the paperwork.

**AWS readiness.** We guide each partner through AWS registration, tax and banking details,
and service-linked role creation.

**A live partner portal.** Onboarding workflow, deal registration with approval and decline
history, and commission tracking against the margin schedule, synchronized with HubSpot or
Salesforce as you specify.

**Enablement and training.** Sessions at your tier cadence covering your product, offer
mechanics, deal registration and the margin schedule.

**A CPPO deal desk.** Partner deal registrations answered to a published turnaround, with
every private offer and CPPO structured and priced ready for issue.

**Selling Authorizations.** Prepared, submitted and tracked to issue.

**Opportunity hygiene.** Partner-sourced opportunities filed to ACE, tracked distinctly, with
AWS Partner Revenue Measurement attribution configured and verified.

**A monthly report.** Delivered within 5 business days of month end, in your brand, in a form
you can pass on unaltered.

## How it runs

1. **Scope.** We agree the ideal partner profile, the tier and the intake channel.
2. **Recruit and onboard.** Candidates screened, contracted, AWS-registered and enabled.
3. **Transact.** Partners register deals through the desk; offers and Selling Authorizations
   go out to the turnaround for your tier.
4. **Report and review.** Monthly pack, plus a channel review at your tier cadence.

## What gets reported

The headline number is **Offer-Issuing Partners** — distinct partners that issued at least one
private offer or CPPO against your listing that month, read from the portal and reconciled
against AWS Marketplace seller reporting.

Alongside it: partners onboarded this month and cumulatively, partners pending AWS
prerequisites, active partners, deal registrations received, approved and declined, deal desk
performance, Selling Authorizations requested and issued, and attributed channel revenue.

## Not included

Bespoke integration work, partner marketing spend, channel incentive funding, legal advice,
and services to your own direct customers. Each is quoted separately.
