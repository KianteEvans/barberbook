## Read this before submitting

**These are drafts written from the deck and the outcomes documents. Nothing here has been
reviewed by either partner, and no AWS submission should go out on them unchecked.**

**Every `[bracketed]` item is unset and must be filled before submission.** They fall into
three groups:

1. **Registry facts neither partner has supplied here** — SFDC and APN SFDC links, partner tier
   and "partner since", business plan owners, office locations, existing competencies and service
   delivery programs, submitter name/phone/email, submission and launch dates.
2. **Work in progress** — the staffing model behind every turnaround and cadence, included volumes
   and overflow rates per rung, and the commercial terms between Automatum and OBP.
3. **Things only AWS or Automatum can confirm** — the 12-month listing clock basis, whose Partner
   Central instance ACE opportunities are filed into, the CPPO boundary, and whether a third-party
   reseller can issue offers against Automatum's listing under its seller-of-record position.

Also still to supply in every study: **Draft Reference Architecture** (a diagram) and
**First Customer Targets** (with ACE opportunity links).

## Two things worth deciding before you submit

**The Accelerator's fit is the weak one.** AWS defines a Business Outcomes Solution as impacting a
customer's top or bottom line, tied to how they make money, and sold to a line-of-business buyer
rather than the IT buyer. The three retainers fit that cleanly. The Accelerator is a fixed-fee
compliance and listing project bought by a CTO or VP Engineering. Study 01 answers "qualified yes"
and asks AWS to assess it as the portfolio's on-ramp. If you would rather not raise it, that answer
is the paragraph to change.

**The Accelerator and Operations Launch overlap.** Operations Launch includes the same WAFR, FTR
and listing publication the Accelerator delivers. Both studies state the pairing: $10,000 for a
listing in 14–18 business days with nothing ongoing, against $18,000 across the term for the
listing plus twelve months of operation.
